They watched Him slip away to pray
alone before the break of day,
and heard Him talk with the Holy One,
not as a servant, but a Son.
"Lord, teach us how to pray," they cried.
"Not with empty words," He replied,
"but come to God the way I do;
call Him 'Abba'—He's your Father too."

Abba, come and reign here,
let what delights You be done,
on earth as it is in heaven,
let Your kingdom come.

I've said these words a thousand times,
a rhythm learned, a string of lines;
I remember fire on Sinai's height,
yet kept my distance from the light.
But the fire still burns and calls me near;
through Your Son, I'm welcome here.
So, Abba, make Your name be known
as holy in my heart of stone.

Abba, come and reign here,

on earth as it is in heaven,
let Your kingdom come.

I've built my barns for years ahead;
teach me to need Your daily bread.
I owed a debt too great to pay;
You nailed the ledger page away.
I'll loose my grip on what I'm owed;
as You released me, I'll let go.
And when the night of testing comes,
rescue me from the evil one.

Abba, come and reign here,
let what delights You be done,
on earth as it is in heaven,
let Your kingdom come.

Not my will, but Yours be done,
the words, through tears, of Your own Son.
Now make His prayer my very own:
take out this heart, this heart of stone;
give me a heart that beats anew,
with Your Spirit breathing through,
till Your delight and mine are one:
not my will, but Yours be done.

Abba, come and reign here,
let what delights You be done,
on earth as it is in heaven,
let it start in me,
let Your kingdom come.

let it start in me,
let Your kingdom come.
