(Psalm 8, Lamentations 3:22-24)

The heavens tell Your glory, Lord,
The skies proclaim what You have done;
Your fingers placed the moon and stars,
You call each morning's rising sun.
Yet who are we that You would care?
Our days are grass, our years a sigh;
We reach for what we cannot hold
Beneath the wonder of Your sky.

But this we call to mind,
And so we hope in You:
Great is Your faithfulness,
Your loving kindness never ends;

You are the Gift of peace,
You are the Breath of life,
All the treasure we could need,
Our hearts' one true desire;
With mercies new each day,
Our portion, 
You're all we seek.

Your voice sustains the turning earth,
You give the seasons, sun and rain;
Each creature looks to You for food,
You open wide Your hand again.
And though the fig tree may not bloom,
Though fortunes fade and kingdoms fall,
We may not touch a single star,
Yet You who set them hold us all.

But this we call to mind,
And so we hope in You:
Great is Your faithfulness,
Your loving kindness never ends;

You are the Gift of peace,
You are the Breath of life,
All the treasure we could need,
Our hearts' one true desire;
With mercies new each day,
Our portion, 
You're all we seek.

Your love is better than my life,
So I will bless Your name;
As long as I have breath,
I'll lift my voice in praise;
When my flesh and heart grow old,
You'll be my portion still;
Each morning, mercies new,
Each morning, praise to You.

You are the Gift of peace,
You are the Breath of life,
All the treasure I could need,
My heart's one true desire;
With mercies new each day,
You're my portion, all I seek.

Yahweh, You're my portion, all I seek.

****
Words by Ron Dougherty
