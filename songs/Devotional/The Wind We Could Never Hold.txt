In this life we labor for many things,
Riches and honor, the treasure of kings;
We fill both our hands, but the heart isn't filled,
Still chasing wind that will never be still.

Now we rise before dawn, every head bowed
To pay and praise, our treasure in the cloud;
We feast on it all, but we're never quite fed,
Still hungry for more, though the table is spread.

Oh, Yahweh is good, taste and see!
Blessed is the one who in Him finds peace;
He gives us true manna that daily feeds,
And His Ruach fills our deepest need.

So we rise before dawn, every head bowed
To the weight of His glory come down in the cloud;
We open our hands, and the heart is now filled,
And the Wind we could never hold holds us still.

Oh, Yahweh is good, taste and see!
Blessed is the one who in Him finds peace;
He gives us true manna that daily feeds,
And His Ruach fills our deepest need.

Every eye is lifted to You,
You give us our bread in its time;
You open Your hand, You open Your hand,
And satisfy all that's alive.

More than the hunger we bring,
More than we knew to desire,
You fill us with favor, You fill us with favor,
Your Ruach, the wind and the fire.

Oh, Yahweh is good, taste and see!
Blessed is the one who in Him finds peace;
He gives us true manna that daily feeds,
And His Ruach fills our deepest need.

Oh, taste and see,
Oh, taste and see.

Words by Ron Dougherty
