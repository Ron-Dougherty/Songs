Two stories tall on a quiet hill,
Trees all around us, the air so still,
Deer in the morning, soft and slow —
We like it simple, 'cause that's what our hearts know.

We left our hill and memories behind,
A small apartment and a future blind —
Four walls, a lease and little more —
Together we were rich, never were we poor.

'Cause we are home, wherever we are,
Under any roof or wandering star,
Love's perfect call still paves the way,
As we grow in grace day after day.
We are home.

Now there's a new house, elegant, clean,
The nicest place that we've ever seen —
But it's just a house 'til it's filled with love,
Filled with you, and grace from above.

'Cause we are home, wherever we are,
Under any roof or wandering star,
Love's perfect call still paves the way,
As we grow in grace day after day.
We are home.

As we grow older, love, we'll be knit as one,
Grace carries us through all things we have done.
Not by chance, not by luck, but by faith we stand —
Forgiven, still walking, still hand in hand.

We were home when we had nothing at all,
We'll be home at last in a Heavenly Hall.
Love's perfect call paved the way,
As we grew in grace day after day.
We are home.

****
Words by Ron Dougherty
